# Illustrations — prompts and file names

Made in the USA: A Plate for Every State · generated from the manuscript.

**How to use.** Generate each image with your AI image tool using the prompt below, then save it with
the exact file name shown in `assets/img/states/<state>/` (e.g. `assets/img/states/alaska/hero.jpg`).
Run `npm run build`: hero landscapes are clipped inside the state outline with the gold rule, food
images fill the food page, and spreads are split across the two facing pages automatically.

**Sizes** are minimums for print at 300 dpi; larger is fine (the layout crops to fit). If your tool
offers fixed aspect ratios, pick the one listed and generate at the highest resolution available, then
upscale if needed.

**Spreads** (two facing pages): keep the main subject off the centre line — the book's spine falls
exactly in the middle of the picture.

**Common style — hero landscapes:** Photorealistic landscape photograph with a subtle painterly finish, natural true-to-season colours, deep perspective, crisp detail, timeless feel for a premium heritage American cookbook: no modern cars, paved roads, power lines or modern signage. Keep exactly the season and light described — do not turn it into a sunset. A full, rectangular landscape photograph that fills the whole frame edge to edge: keep the main subject near the centre and the horizon in the upper third, with nothing important near the edges (the layout will crop it). Do not draw any map, outline, silhouette or cut-out shape.

**Common style — food:** Bon Appétit–style editorial food photography, photorealistic, relaxed home-cooked styling, real and a little imperfect (crumbs, a drip, a used spoon, a portion already served). Not glossy, not restaurant plating, no studio flash. Natural light only, early morning or late afternoon. Composition: one clear focal point on the dish, placed on a rule-of-thirds intersection rather than dead centre; generous negative space; leading lines and shapes (cutlery, a folded napkin, the edge of a board) guiding the eye to the dish; layers and depth with an out-of-focus ingredient in the foreground, the dish, and soft props behind; a balanced asymmetrical arrangement; a little repetition (a scatter of herbs, berries or slices) and a sense of movement (rising steam, a drip, scattered crumbs). Only props and ingredients that belong to the recipe, on a natural surface; a harmonious colour palette with one complementary accent colour; shallow depth of field with the focal point tack-sharp.

**Negative prompt (all images):** No people, no hands, no faces, no text, no lettering, no logos, no labels or packaging with writing (plain unlabeled bottles and jars only), no watermark, no frame or border.

**Reference photos.** Alabama, Alaska and Arizona have a reference landscape from the client
(`illustrations/references/`, licensed for this use): attach it to the prompt in the image tool.

**Round 5 (client feedback):** food uses early-morning or late-afternoon natural light and the
composition rules the client listed; Alabama, Alaska and Arizona get new hero landscapes from the
reference photos. All earlier pictures follow older briefs and should be regenerated.
